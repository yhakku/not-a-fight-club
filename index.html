Your task now is to select one zone to attack and two zones to defend. Click on the parts of your opponent’s body that you want to strike — this is your attack. Then choose two zones you want to defend — you’ll try to block your enemy’s hits.

After choosing, press "Attack!" to exchange blows. Protected zones block damage; if unprotected, your health drops. Critical hits can deal extra damage and break defenses. A detailed battle log will appear below for each turn.

Good luck in your fight!
